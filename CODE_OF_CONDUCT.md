# DPN Technology Community Standards

DPN Technology public project spaces are for productive technical collaboration.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss people and ideas professionally;
- critique code, designs and decisions rather than attacking individuals;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination and deliberate disruption;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information;
- keep technical disagreement evidence-based;
- follow repository security and disclosure rules.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content that materially disrupts collaboration, exposes sensitive information or violates these standards.
